This is the initial-pass ledger. Follow-up additions, corrected source identities, replaced historical material, all 380 issue checks, and the portrait audit are recorded in [the completeness follow-up](platform-completeness-audit.md).

Scope: all 38 listed candidacies, including write-ins. Ten issue categories each. The same reader layout and source-link behavior as Crow is used. All research remains draft and unapproved; automated evidence review is not human approval.

Sources are candidate campaign statements, official statements, attributed reporting, and candidate directories. Statements of intent are not proof of policy effectiveness or enactment. Undated and historical material does not establish current 2026 applicability. Party platforms, endorsements, allegations, campaign statistics, and material about other candidates are excluded from the summaries.
