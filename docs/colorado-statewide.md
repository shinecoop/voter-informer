# Colorado 2026 statewide executive races

Added statewide candidate lists for Governor (`/2026/colorado/governor/candidates`), Secretary of State (`/2026/colorado/secretary-of-state/candidates`), and Treasurer (`/2026/colorado/treasurer/candidates`). Browse races → Colorado offers each office directly, without a district step. No platform pages or platform links are provided for these races.

The [official Colorado general-election roster](https://www.sos.state.co.us/pubs/elections/vote/generalCandidates.html), retrieved with Firecrawl on October 30, 2026, lists seven Governor tickets, five Secretary of State candidates, and four State Treasurer candidates. All are printed-ballot candidates; none of these three sections lists certified write-ins. The source says it reflects September 4 certification. Governor tickets preserve the adjacent lieutenant governor names and parties from the official roster.

The existing list design, first-name sorting, source disclosure, and unavailable-detail labels apply. Ages and photographs for these newly added people have not been verified and are marked unavailable. Campaign website associations use the election directories below, independently of ballot verification. A missing campaign link means unverified, not absent.
