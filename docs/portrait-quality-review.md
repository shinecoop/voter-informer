# Profile-photo presentation review — 2026-10-30

All 38 profile slots were reviewed using the 35 source photographs already gathered. This is a visual presentation judgment, not independent authentication or a claim about a candidate’s professionalism.

Criteria: a readable face, sufficient detail at profile size, appropriate crop, even exposure, and a professional presentation. Studio photography and formal dress are not required; suitable outdoor and workplace portraits are retained. Extreme crops, poor webcam/video captures, low-angle selfies, dim flyer extracts, and overly casual snapshots are withheld.

Nine photographs were withheld. Twenty-six remain in use; twelve profiles use the shared default silhouette, including the three previously without an identified photo. The same fallback is used in candidate lists and platform headers. Withheld assets were moved out of `public/` into the ignored research archive, retaining provenance and file hashes in `data/portrait-research.json`. No policy summaries or review/approval states were changed.
