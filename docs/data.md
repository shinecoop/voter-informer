# Research data contract

`data/research.json` must contain all thirteen collections, even when empty. Unknown fields are rejected. IDs are stable lowercase keys, unique within each collection; questions use unique `(id, version)` pairs so earlier wording is preserved.

The schema covers geography and boundary provenance; election stages; House/district and Senate/state races; people separately from candidacies and dated party/ballot status; versioned policy questions; ordered local-question selections and sourced rationales; dated position evidence; votes/actions separately from outcomes; source metadata; category-specific freshness; coverage manifests; and comparable historical presidential results.

Source links must use HTTP(S). Sources require publisher, type, retrieval date, title and locator; unknown publication/event dates are explicitly `null`. Dates use calendar-valid ISO `YYYY-MM-DD`. Every sourced reference and relationship must resolve. A position's question version must apply to its candidacy's race. Geography must include boundary vintage, source IDs, geometry reference, and explicit county-overlap metadata. Geometry references are identifiers only: the foundation never fetches or opens them.

Public editorial records (positions, record entries, local selections, coverage, historical results) require `publication: "published"` and `review.state: "reviewed"` with nonempty reviewer and review date. Drafts remain excluded by the repository. Policy labels additionally require review even in drafts: `supports`, `opposes`, `conditional-mixed`, or `no-explicit-position-found`. For an unreviewed draft use `label: null`. Position entries carry research scope and date, including when no explicit position was found. A label cannot be derived from party or affiliation. These gates validate recorded review metadata; they cannot establish whether an actual human performed accurate review.

Identity/ballot/source records have no editorial publication flag; all are public through the repository. Do not put private notes, reviewer contact details, credentials, or internal submissions in any research collection. Freshness records may include an internal reviewer reference, so use non-sensitive editor identifiers only. Publication filtering is not a confidentiality boundary for version-controlled files or deployment source.

Coverage other than unavailable requires geography/race scope, verification sources and a verification date. Completeness still needs editorial verification against official ballot records; validation cannot prove an official roster is exhaustive. Historical overlays can publish only with verified comparable boundary vintage and vote shares totaling at most one. The schema does not verify source truth, licensing, or causal claims.

Run `npm run validate:data` after every edit. `npm run build` runs the same gate and fails closed. Unit tests keep synthetic data separate and exercise bad references, dates, labels, review, geography, versions, and safe repository failures. Review intervals and due/overdue UI are later milestone decisions; freshness storage supports unknown checks, category, interval, and next-review date now.


Candidate identity records now optionally support `birthDate` (ISO date or known year-month), `birthDateSourceId`, and a local JPEG `portrait` with source, credit, and an optional permitted license URL. A date requires a resolving provenance source; portraits require a resolving source and stay under `/portraits/`. Month-only birth dates yield no age during that month, since the exact birthday is unknown. Ages are otherwise calculated on the election date, not cached as a timeless property. Missing ages and photographs are presented explicitly.

The `certified-write-in` candidacy status distinguishes certified write-ins from names printed on the ballot. Lists include both and retain withdrawn entries if documented later. The 2026 Colorado general-election roster was checked against the Secretary of State list on October 24, 2026; its source was last updated September 15. First-name ordering follows the owner's latest instruction. No candidate platform summaries, recommendations, or human-review claims were added.

Candidacies optionally include `campaignWebsite: { url, sourceId }`. URLs must use HTTP(S), and the provenance source must resolve. Candidate lists display the hostname without leading `www.` beneath the name while preserving the full URL as the destination. Missing URLs are omitted without placeholders. The current data includes 26 campaign website associations from the linked public campaign directory; the other 12 remain unverified, not confirmed absent. These links do not imply endorsement or verification of the websites’ policy claims.
