# Colorado 2026 U.S. Senate candidate list

The statewide list is `/2026/colorado/us-senate/candidates`. Browse races → Colorado → U.S. Senate opens it directly, without a district step. Its breadcrumbs and race switcher use the same navigation as House lists. Senate names are plain text; no Senate platform routes, preview routes, or platform research were added.

## Roster and provenance

Checked October 30, 2026 using Firecrawl against the [Colorado Secretary of State official general-election list](https://www.sos.state.co.us/pubs/elections/vote/generalCandidates.html), which says it reflects certification to counties on September 4. Six names are printed on the ballot and three are certified write-ins. The list uses the existing first-name ordering and election-date age calculation (November 3, 2026).

This directory is not used for ballot qualification or policy claims. Other campaign links remain unverified.

Hickenlooper's birth date, February 7, 1952, comes from his [official congressional biography](https://bioguide.congress.gov/search/bio/H000273). Baisley's birth date, August 2, 1955, comes from his [Wikipedia biography](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mark_Baisley). All other ages are marked unavailable.

Hickenlooper's [official Senate portrait](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:John_Hickenlooper,_official_portrait,_117th_Congress.jpeg) is credited to Renee Bouchard, United States Senate Photographic Studio, and marked public domain as a federal government work. The local JPEG is Wikimedia's 500px rendition, displayed with a circular CSS crop. Other portraits remain unavailable; Baisley's Commons image had inconsistent description/licensing metadata and was not used.
